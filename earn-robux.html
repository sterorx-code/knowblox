Gamehag Review (2026)

Gamehag is a veteran GPT (Get-Paid-To) platform dating back to 2016 (originally "Earnweb"), with a gamified UI, frequent offer updates, and a wide mix of tasks: playing games, surveys, and shopping offers. It runs on Soul Gems—earn them via offerwalls and redeem for cash, gift cards, Steam wallet codes, or Steam game keys. The rough baseline is 1,000 Soul Gems ≈ $1.00, though Steam-related redemptions can vary. A notable perk: because Gamehag is tied to Rust Clash Entertainment (Clash.gg, RustClash, CasesGG), you can transfer gems to those sites at a +25% rate (1,000 SG → $1.25 balance). There's also a mobile app with offers for both iOS and Android users.

Earning extras include an hourly Rain (equal split among chat participants who've completed at least one offer) and a weekly leaderboard worth 100,000 Soul Gems ($100) to top earners. Unlike many GPT competitors, there's no sign-up or daily login bonus.

Payout breadth is solid, covering real money, gift cards, and Steam keys. Ownership and ops are public: RCPE Ventures LTD (Cyprus) owns Gamehag; Lootably, Inc. (Poland) operates it day-to-day. Reputation is mixed-positive—roughly ~250k monthly visits, 3.6/5 on Trustpilot (9k+ reviews), but some 2024-2025 feedback cites revoked gems, unclear bans, and slow support.

Rules & safety: VPNs/proxies are prohibited (ban/forfeit risk), and users report occasional false positives. If you stick to legit offers and avoid VPNs, Gamehag remains a versatile, item-friendly GPT option—especially for Steam redemptions.
